Blade product scrap rate, drawn to scale

When parts fail, a customer is waiting. I run 8D investigations, find the real root cause and put corrective actions in place, usually within about a month.

Investigations I personally led

  • Blade scrap, brought down from 14% to 1.8%
  • Weld failures between tip and tube

  • Laser marks rubbing off
  • Several cleaning-related part issues

Spotlight: glue residue

  • Glue residue and taping problems were disrupting downstream processes
  • Traced the root cause to a supplier changing its adhesive formulation
  • Updated the customer daily and resolved it in under a month, with no production delay

07 · TEGRA MEDICAL · NOWDOEProcess development

Making laser and grinding processes repeatable

My roleManufacturing engineer running the experiments, and PM for tube forming
What I owned

DOE design and analysis, fixture and grinding changes, and the closed-end tube forming project plan

Who I led and coordinated

Production and the laser and grinding operators

New parts fail on the details: a mark too shallow to read, a slot cut a few degrees off the needle point, a tube end that won't close cleanly. I run the experiments and fixture changes that turn these into stable, documented processes.

Current work

  • Laser marking DOEs: characterized marking parameters for a 2 mm font; now running a second DOE for a smaller font that isn't marking deep enough, working within the laser's fixed 50 kHz frequency and its focus settings around the focal plane
  • Slot cutting and needle point grinding: leading the effort to make the laser slot sit in the same orientation relative to the ground needle point every time, through grinding process and fixture changes
  • Closed-end tube forming: project manager for a development project to form closed tube ends
